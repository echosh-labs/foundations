# Foundational Story: A Journey of Healing and Revelation

## Narrative Overview
- **Event and Context**: Circa September 11th, 2008.
- **Background**: Characterized by clinical staff as a manic episode resulting from severe sleep deprivation, leading to a state of heightened visceral awareness.

## The Core Revelation
- **Interconnectedness**: Visceral and frightening realization of deep, profound interconnectedness with other human beings.
- **Existential Fear**: Generated deep existential dread regarding the risk of high-level control or central command by powerful "chosen" individuals over the lives and thoughts of many.

## Confession, Healing, and Reconciliation
- **Remorse**: A deeply personal confession of sorrow and immense responsibility over the fear of having seriously endangered his infant son, Luke, by dropping him into a fire.
- **Family Restoration**: Deep expression of love for Luke (now in his early 20s) and acknowledgment of the profound healing that has taken place within the family unit over the years.
- **Purpose**: This narrative serves as the cornerstone and foundational theme for an upcoming professional production.
